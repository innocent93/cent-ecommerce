## 1. Why this policy exists, and who it binds

Because UrbanStep is a marketplace, every order involves three parties with different
responsibilities: **you** (the customer), the **seller** who owns and shipped the product, and
**UrbanStep**, which processes the payment and mediates disputes. This policy sets one consistent
standard across all sellers so your rights don't vary listing to listing.

in Section 3. This runs from the platform's tracked delivery date, not from when the seller

## 4. How a refund is decided — who's responsible for what

1. **You submit a refund request** through your order page, with a reason and (for
   damage/defect/wrong-item claims) photos.
2. **The seller is notified first** and has [X days] to respond — approve, propose a
   replacement, or dispute the claim.
3. **If the seller doesn't respond, or the claim is disputed, UrbanStep reviews it directly** and
   makes a binding decision. This is what "UrbanStep mediates disputes" means in practice — you
   are never stuck waiting indefinitely on an unresponsive seller.
4. **Approved refunds** are issued to your original payment method, typically within
   [X business days] of approval.

**Who actually pays for an approved refund:** the seller's portion of that sale (the amount they
were owed after commission) is deducted from what they're paid — a refund is never simply
absorbed by UrbanStep while the seller keeps their earnings. This is why payouts are only released
for orders past the refund window (see the Terms of Service, Section 9) — it avoids clawing money
back from a seller after it's already been paid out, which is the most common source of
marketplace payout disputes.

## 7. Order cancellations before shipping

You may cancel an order for a full refund any time before the seller marks it as shipped. Once
shipped, Section 2–4 above apply instead.

## 8. What happens to the product

For an approved return, the seller may request the item be sent back before the refund is
finalized, or may waive the return (e.g. for low-value items where return shipping would exceed
the refund itself) — this is communicated to you as part of the resolution in Section 4.
