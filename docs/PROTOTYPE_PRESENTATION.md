# UrbanStep Client Prototype Presentation

## 1. What the prototype is

UrbanStep is a multi-vendor fashion marketplace prototype showing the complete customer, seller and admin journey. The prototype is designed to communicate the product experience before final production rollout.

## 2. 8-minute client demo flow

1. **Brand promise (30 sec):** Start on the home page. Explain that UrbanStep is designed as a curated fashion marketplace rather than a generic catalogue.
2. **Discovery (60 sec):** Search, category navigation, filters, product cards, wishlist and product details.
3. **Conversion (90 sec):** Add an item, review cart, choose delivery, apply a coupon, select Paystack/card/bank transfer and place the order.
4. **Trust (45 sec):** Show seller information, reviews, returns and order tracking.
5. **Account (45 sec):** Show orders, addresses, wishlist and notifications.
6. **Seller (90 sec):** Register, upload a business certificate, show pending verification, then show the approved seller dashboard, products, stock, orders, earnings and payouts.
7. **Admin (90 sec):** Show analytics, seller approval queue, certificate review, products, orders, customers, coupons, refunds, payouts and staff controls.
8. **Close (30 sec):** Explain that production hardening includes live payment credentials, cloud storage, monitoring, backups, security testing and deployment validation.

## 3. Prototype acceptance criteria

The client should approve these areas before production:

- Brand direction and typography
- Desktop/mobile responsiveness
- Customer purchase flow
- Seller onboarding and verification
- Admin operations
- Refund and return experience
- Order tracking
- Analytics and reporting
- Empty/loading/error states
- Accessibility and keyboard navigation

## 4. How to price the work

A sensible Nigerian market structure is to separate the prototype from production implementation:

- Visual/interactive prototype: **₦300,000–₦650,000**
- Connected coded prototype: **₦650,000–₦1,200,000+**
- Full multi-vendor MVP: price separately after scope, integrations and deployment requirements are signed off.

Do not include indefinite maintenance in the prototype fee. Quote maintenance/support as a separate monthly or quarterly agreement.

## 5. Client presentation language

> “UrbanStep is being designed as a fashion-first marketplace: one storefront for customers, a focused operating workspace for sellers, and a control center for the platform. The prototype validates the customer journey and operational model before we lock the production implementation.”
