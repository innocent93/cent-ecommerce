## 1. What this covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data UrbanStep ("we") collects from **customers** and
**sellers**, why, and — because this is a marketplace, not a single-party store — **who else that
data is shared with and why**, which is the part most templates skip.

## 2. Data we collect

**From customers:** name, email, phone, delivery address(es), order history, and payment
metadata (we use Paystack for payment processing — we do not store full card numbers; see
Section 5). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from
Google.

**From sellers:** business name, owner name, email, phone, bank account details (for payouts,
also processed via Paystack), and product listing content.

**Automatically:** IP address, device/browser type, and basic usage logs, for security and
fraud prevention (see Section 6).

## 3. How we use it

- To process and fulfill orders, including routing each order to the correct seller.
- To calculate and pay out seller commissions.
- To communicate order status, respond to support requests, and send policy or security notices.
- To detect and prevent fraud, and to enforce these Terms.
- [If applicable: to send marketing communications — state opt-in/opt-out mechanism.]

## 4. Who we share it with — the marketplace-specific part

**Sellers see:** for an order placed with them, the customer's name, delivery address, and the
items/quantities ordered — the minimum needed to fulfill that order. A seller does **not** see a
customer's full order history with other sellers, payment details, or account credentials.

**Customers see:** a seller's business name and, where provided, publicly listed contact/return
information — not a seller's bank details, commission rate, or other business data.

**Payment processor (Paystack):** processes payments and payouts; see Paystack's own privacy
policy for how they handle payment data. We do not store full card numbers ourselves.

**Google (only if you use Google Sign-In):** we send Google's servers a token to verify your
identity; we do not share your UrbanStep activity with Google in the other direction.

**Service providers:** [cloud hosting, email/SMS delivery, error tracking — list yours, e.g.
"MongoDB Atlas (database hosting), Cloudinary (image hosting), Sentry (error monitoring)"] —
each only receives what's necessary to perform their function, under contract to protect it.

**We do not sell personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.**

## 5. Payment data

Card and bank details are handled directly by Paystack, our PCI-compliant payment processor —
UrbanStep's own servers never receive or store full card numbers. Seller bank account details are
stored to register with Paystack as a transfer recipient for payouts.
